The Day Everything Started ❤️

We never planned to take this picture, and maybe that’s what makes it so special. ❤️

This photo holds the memory of the day when everything quietly started. That day, we ended up being on the same team for the game and the dance. It felt like just another normal day at the time, but looking back now, it feels like something much more.

Because if we hadn’t been on the same team that day, maybe we would have never talked, never known each other, and none of these beautiful memories would exist. Sometimes, the most important moments in our lives happen without us even realizing their importance.

Everything started from this day, with a simple team, a simple moment, and two people who didn’t know how special they would become to each other. ✨❤️
